Email alerts

We send two kinds of alerts: "hot day" and "starting to run."

A "hot day" fires when a boat you follow has today's avg fish/angler more than 2× its

A "starting to run" fires when a species you follow has a rolling 7-day fleet-wide

alert per species per week, max.

historically, conditions matched a pattern.
