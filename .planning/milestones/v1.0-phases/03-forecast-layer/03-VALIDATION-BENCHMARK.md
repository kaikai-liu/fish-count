## Methodology

Two variants are compared on every (date, species, trip_type) cell in the held-out year where actual outcomes exist:

1. **Seasonal-naïve baseline (shipped):** weighted yield = SUM(species_count) / SUM(angler_count) over rows from prior years where source_date is within ±7 calendar days of the same (month, day-of-month). Empirical 10/90 percentiles of per-trip per-angler ratios form the 80% prediction interval.
2. **Fleet-mean baseline (comparison):** weighted yield = SUM(species_count) / SUM(angler_count) over ALL prior-year rows for the same (species, trip_type), no seasonal window.

Actuals = SUM(species_count) / NULLIF(SUM(angler_count), 0) per held-out (date, species, trip_type).
